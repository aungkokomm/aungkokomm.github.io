PocketReader

Pocket is gone — your reading list shouldn't be. Save articles in Raindrop.io like always; PocketReader pulls them down, archives each one's full text and images on your own disk, and gives you a calm place to read, search, highlight and revisit them — online or off. No subscription, no server, no cloud. One portable folder you actually own.

Why it exists

For a decade, "read it later" meant Pocket — save now, read later, trust it'll be there. Then Mozilla shut it down for good. Raindrop.io is the natural place to save now (unlimited bookmarks, free), but the reading half has walls: full-text search, permanent copies of your articles and annotations are all locked behind Pro, and everything opens online-only — one dead link or new paywall and the article you saved is gone. PocketReader fills that gap. Point it at your Raindrop account once and your library becomes yours: it reads beautifully offline, it's searchable to the last word, and it outlives link-rot and paywalls.

Everything the reading half needs — free and offline

The features Raindrop charges for, done locally and for free — plus a lot it doesn't do at all.

  • Full-text search across every cached article, offline and instant. Raindrop: Pro-only.
  • Forever copies of your articles — full text and images, on your own disk. Raindrop: Pro-only.
  • Annotations — in-text highlights and a private note on every article. Raindrop: Pro-only.
  • Smart tags — suggestions and auto-tagging, computed on your machine. Raindrop: Pro-only.
  • And things Raindrop doesn't do: offline text-to-speech, export to PDF, reading statistics, 1–5 star ratings, reading-progress & resume, and a Recycle Bin.

Why PocketReader

📴 Truly offline

It caches the full article text and images to your disk, so reading, searching and highlighting all work with no connection — and survive link-rot and paywalls.

🔎 Full-text search

Search the words inside every cached article, not just titles and tags — across tens of thousands of articles in milliseconds.

🖊 Highlights & notes

Highlight text right in the reader and jot a private note per article. Both are saved locally and reappear every time you open it.

🔊 Listen & export

Built-in offline text-to-speech reads any article aloud, and one click exports it to a clean PDF exactly as it reads.

⭐ Organize & track

Star ratings, a drag-and-drop tag manager, reading progress with resume, a statistics page, and browse by Favorites, Unread, Notes, Highlights or any tag.

🔒 One-way & private

It only ever reads from Raindrop and never writes back. No telemetry, no external AI — your ratings, notes and highlights stay on your machine.

How it works

  • Install. Run the setup and pick any folder or drive — even a USB stick. No administrator rights needed.
  • Connect Raindrop. The simplest way is Sign in with a token (Raindrop → Settings → Integrations → create a test token). It takes a minute and never expires. Browser OAuth is also supported if you supply your own app credentials.
  • Sync. Pull your bookmarks in — the first sync grabs everything, after that it only fetches what changed.
  • Download offline. Cache the full articles, then read, search and highlight anywhere, anytime.

Good to know

📥 Install it, or carry it

One self-contained folder — no Docker, no server to babysit. Install to any drive or run it portable from a USB stick. It isn't code-signed, so if SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

🔑 A free Raindrop account is all you need

No PocketReader account, no second device. Sign in once with a Raindrop token — it never leaves your computer, and it only lets PocketReader read your bookmarks.

🧳 Move it anywhere

Your articles, tags, notes, highlights and settings all live in the folder next to the app. Copy that folder to a new PC or drive and your whole library travels with it.
