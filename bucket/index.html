Bucket

A staging shelf for moving files around Windows. Gather files, folders, text, images and links from anywhere into a little always-on-top bucket — then copy, move, zip, or drag them out wherever you need.

⬇ Download v1.0.4 View source
C# / WinUI 3 · .NET 10 · Portable · MIT licensed

Why Bucket

🪣 Collect anything

Drag in, paste, or use the Add button — files, folders, text snippets, images, and links. Text, images and links are saved as real files for you.

🤙 Summon it anywhere

Shake the mouse to pop a bucket under your cursor, press Ctrl+Shift+B, or drop onto the optional screen-edge catcher.

🎨 Many buckets

Open as many as you like — each its own colour, nameable, with compact and expanded square modes that toggle with a smooth animation.

📤 Deposit in one move

Copy To… / Move To… any folder with conflict handling and progress, pin Quick Destinations, or drag straight out to Explorer or any app (Shift = move).

🗜 Export transforms

Copy the whole bucket as a .zip, flatten subfolders into one place, or batch-rename and copy with a sequential prefix.

🔒 Safe & private

Adding an item only stores a reference — nothing is copied, moved, or deleted until you say so. System-tray resident, portable, no telemetry.

Getting started

  • Run it — portable. Download the latest release and launch it; it sits in the system tray, ready whenever you need it.
  • Summon a bucket. Shake the mouse to pop one under your cursor, press Ctrl+Shift+B, or drop onto the optional screen-edge catcher.
  • Fill it from anywhere. Drag in, paste, or use Add — files, folders, text, images and links. Text, images and links are saved as real files for you.
  • Deposit when ready. Copy To… / Move To… any folder, zip the whole bucket, or drag items straight out to Explorer or any app (hold Shift to move).

Good to know

🔒 References only

Adding an item just stores a reference — nothing is copied, moved or deleted until you choose an action. Your files stay exactly where they are.

📥 Portable & private

System-tray resident, runs from any folder, no telemetry. It isn't code-signed, so if SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

🪟 Windows 10 & 11

Runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. Built with C# / WinUI 3 on .NET 10.
