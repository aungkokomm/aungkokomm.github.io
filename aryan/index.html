Aryan eBook Library

Every book on every drive, in one library that reads them all. Most eBook managers copy your books into a library of their own and rename them on the way. Aryan leaves every file exactly where it is: point it at your folders and it builds one library from all of them, with covers, authors and series, and reads every book in its own reader. Free, open source, and it works offline.

What's new in v1.5.0: many books at once, and tools for big libraries ⚡

Ticking books is quick now, and a library in the thousands can be searched, sorted and marked from the keyboard.

  • Tick many books, quickly. Ctrl+click ticks a book and Shift+click ticks every book from the last one ticked, or point at a cover and click the tick in its corner. Space, Shift+arrows and Ctrl+A do it from the keyboard.
  • One menu for all of them. Right-click a ticked book to mark, favorite, list, edit, look up or copy every ticked book at once.
  • Search by field. author:tolstoy, tag:history, year:1990-2000, rating:4+, format:pdf, is:unread, and a minus to leave books out: -tag:lent. Save as shelf keeps any such search.
  • Go anywhere with Ctrl+K. Type a few letters to jump to any book, author, series, tag, list, shelf or page.
  • Rate and mark from the keyboard. 1 to 5 give a book stars, F makes it a favorite, R marks it reading and D finished.
  • Copy to a folder or drive. Put the ticked books on an e-reader or a USB stick; the originals stay where they are.
  • More ways to sort and see. Sort by progress, size or format, and choose the list's columns: rating, status, file size, date added and last opened.

🛠️ Also recent: adding folders and updating, on every PC (v1.4.3)

  • Adding a folder works on every PC. On some PCs, Windows' folder window failed before it even opened. Aryan now uses the newer folder and file windows that run inside Aryan itself.
  • Updating goes back to your copy. The installer finds the Aryan you already have, portable or installed, and updates it there with your library kept.

🔊 Also recent: read aloud, bookmarks, and fewer steps (v1.4.0)

Most of it came from a reader's notes on what slowed their reading down.

  • Read aloud. Press Ctrl+Shift+U or the speaker button and Windows' own voices read the book, with nothing sent online. The spoken word is marked and the page turns with it, and Hindi is read in a Hindi voice. Windows has no Burmese voice yet, so Burmese books can't be read aloud.
  • Open a book in one step. Point at a cover for Read, or press Enter. Alt+Enter shows the book's details.
  • Bookmarks. Ctrl+D or the ribbon button marks the page. Bookmarks are listed with your highlights, and F2 steps through them.
  • Pages at a glance. PDFs and comics show every page as a small picture, and a PDF without its own table of contents opens on its pages.
  • Text and layout. Choose the font, line spacing and text width for books, and justified text if you like it.
  • Keys for everything. Contents, pages and highlights (Ctrl+Shift+C, P, H), the next chapter (Ctrl+Right), the page colour (Ctrl+Shift+T), and Page Up, Page Down, Home and End in the library. F1 lists them all.
  • A quicker start. Your books are on screen before Aryan checks your folders for changes.

🎨 Also recent: back to your library, in your colours (v1.2.0)

  • Back to the library. Every reader has a Library button at the left of its toolbar. It closes the book and brings your library back. Ctrl+W does the same.
  • Colour themes. Pick one of eight colours in Settings > Appearance. It colours buttons and selections and tints every window, in light and dark, as strongly as you like. Your books' pages are never tinted, and Aryan blue keeps the classic look.
  • Know when there's a new version. When it starts, Aryan asks GitHub whether a newer version is out, and a small bubble offers it. Nothing downloads by itself, and nothing about you or your books is sent.

Why Aryan

📚 Every drive, one library

Add the folders you keep books in, on any drive. Unplug a drive and its books stay in the library, marked offline. Move or rename a file in File Explorer and Aryan finds it again at the next scan.

📖 A reader for every book

PDFs stay sharp at any zoom. EPUB, MOBI, AZW3 and KFX books scroll straight through from chapter to chapter. Comics, whether CBZ, CBR or picture EPUBs, run in one continuous column.

🌏 Made for Myanmar and Hindi readers

Right-click an English word for its meaning, with the Myanmar and Hindi translations right underneath. Burmese titles in Zawgyi are shown as proper Unicode.

🖍️ Highlights, clips and notes

Highlight in five colours, clip any part of a page as a picture, and write notes. One page gathers them from every book, and one click takes you back to the page.

🧭 Pick up where you left off

A Now reading card and a Continue reading row keep your current books first, every book reopens at your page, and the reading log tells you roughly how long a book has left.

✨ Details that fill themselves

Titles, authors, series and covers come from the book itself, Calibre's metadata, the copyright page and even the file name. Online lookups only fill the gaps, and only if you turn them on.

🧹 A library that stays tidy

Find duplicate copies, books whose files have gone missing, and books that still need details. Edit many books at once, and sort them into lists, shelves, series and tags.

⚡ Quick, even when it's big

A library of 2,800 books is on screen in under three seconds, and scans after the first one only look at what changed.

Read it your way

Right-click any word for its meaning in English, Myanmar and Hindi. No internet needed.

PDFs stay sharp at any zoom, with one zoom button in every reader.

Good to know

📥 Install it, or carry it

Choose Install for me (no administrator rights needed) or Portable, into any folder or USB drive. It isn't code-signed, so if SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

🔒 Your books stay yours

No account, no tracking. Aryan never changes, renames or moves a book file, and your favourites, lists and highlights travel with each drive.

⌨️ Every key at hand

The library and every reader can be driven from the keyboard. Press F1 to see every shortcut, and Ctrl+K in the library to go anywhere.

About the name

Aryan (आर्यन) is my son's name; he is one year old. In India and across South Asia it is a common given name, meaning "noble" in Sanskrit and Hindi. The app has nothing to do with the racist misuse of the word in 20th-century Europe, which I reject completely.
