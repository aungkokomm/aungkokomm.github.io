Aryan eBook Library

Every book on every drive, in one library that reads them all. Most eBook managers copy your books into a library of their own and rename them on the way. Aryan leaves every file exactly where it is: point it at your folders and it builds one library from all of them, with covers, authors and series, and reads every book in its own reader. Free, open source, and it works offline.

What's new in v1.1.0: Kindle's newest books, KFX 📚

KFX is the format Amazon uses for Kindle books today, and almost nothing outside Amazon's own apps can read it. Aryan now can.

  • KFX books in your library. Aryan finds them when it scans your folders and lists them under Kindle, with their titles, authors and covers, Burmese books included.
  • Read like any other book. A KFX book opens in the book reader with Define in English, Myanmar and Hindi, highlights and notes, text size and the reading log.
  • Your files stay as they are. Each book gets a readable copy, made once and kept in Aryan's data folder. KFX books bought from the Kindle Store are locked with DRM, and Aryan says so instead of trying to open them.

📖 Also recent: a guide for your first hour (v1.0.11)

  • The getting-started guide. Thirteen short sections: install Aryan, bring in your books, read comfortably, look up words, highlight, keep track of your reading, and back it all up.
  • One click from the app. Settings > About opens the guide, right above the website and source code links.

📜 Also recent: books that scroll on (v1.0.9)

  • Scroll through a whole book. In Scroll layout the mouse wheel carries you from one chapter into the next, so an EPUB reads as one long page.
  • One zoom button in every reader. PDF, comic and EPUB readers share one button with a slider, step buttons and a one-click way back to Fit width, which stays the default.
  • Picture books read as comics (v1.0.8). Graphic novels made of page pictures open one page at a time, fit to width, scrolling.

Why Aryan

📚 Every drive, one library

Add the folders you keep books in, on any drive. Unplug a drive and its books stay in the library, marked offline. Move or rename a file in File Explorer and Aryan finds it again at the next scan.

📖 A reader for every book

PDFs stay sharp at any zoom. EPUB, MOBI, AZW3 and KFX books scroll straight through from chapter to chapter. Comics, whether CBZ, CBR or picture EPUBs, run in one continuous column.

🌏 Made for Myanmar and Hindi readers

Right-click an English word for its meaning, with the Myanmar and Hindi translations right underneath. Burmese titles in Zawgyi are shown as proper Unicode.

🖍️ Highlights, clips and notes

Highlight in five colours, clip any part of a page as a picture, and write notes. One page gathers them from every book, and one click takes you back to the page.

🧭 Pick up where you left off

A Now reading card and a Continue reading row keep your current books first, every book reopens at your page, and the reading log tells you roughly how long a book has left.

✨ Details that fill themselves

Titles, authors, series and covers come from the book itself, Calibre's metadata, the copyright page and even the file name. Online lookups only fill the gaps, and only if you turn them on.

🧹 A library that stays tidy

Find duplicate copies, books whose files have gone missing, and books that still need details. Edit many books at once, and sort them into lists, shelves, series and tags.

⚡ Quick, even when it's big

A library of 2,800 books is on screen in under three seconds, and scans after the first one only look at what changed.

Read it your way

Right-click any word for its meaning in English, Myanmar and Hindi. No internet needed.

PDFs stay sharp at any zoom, with one zoom button in every reader.

Good to know

📥 Install it, or carry it

Choose Install for me (no administrator rights needed) or Portable, into any folder or USB drive. It isn't code-signed, so if SmartScreen warns you, click More info, then Run anyway.

🔒 Your books stay yours

No account, no tracking. Aryan never changes, renames or moves a book file, and your favourites, lists and highlights travel with each drive.

⌨️ Every key at hand

Every reader can be driven from the keyboard. Press F1 in any reader to see every shortcut.

About the name

Aryan (आर्यन) is my son's name; he is one year old. In India and across South Asia it is a common given name, meaning "noble" in Sanskrit and Hindi. The app has nothing to do with the racist misuse of the word in 20th-century Europe, which I reject completely.
